FIFA world cup

world cup is a football tournament that takes place every four years. It is organized by FIFA and features teams from around the world competing for the title of world champion. The tournament is held in different countries each time, and it attracts millions of fans from all over the globe. The teams that participate in the world cup are selected through a qualification process that takes place over several years. The tournament consists of a group stage followed by knockout rounds, culminating in the final match where the winner is crowned world champion.
